# NETRA Consumer & Creator Prototype

A mobile-first NETRA experience with separate Consumer and Creator workspaces. The creator workspace adds creator onboarding, Create Studio, CFund Arena, consumer intelligence, and a reputation profile.

On a fresh browser, NETRA opens with a one-time cinematic campaign story before the existing welcome and onboarding screens. Swipe, tap a dot, or let slides advance every 4.5 seconds; Launch NETRA and Skip both continue into the original onboarding. The story completion flag is saved in this browser.

## Open it

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No install or build step is needed. Plus Jakarta Sans loads from Google Fonts when the browser is online.

## What’s included

- Consumer, Creator, and Brand entry options, plus a simple create-account form with password confirmation and show/hide controls
- A Home feed with a looping Daily Mix background video, creator stories, 12 business cards, creator notes, products near the selected budget, trending topics, vertical Bite previews, and ad categories
- A full-height, swipeable Bites feed with clip-style motion and controls
- Ad Critiques, paid feedback examples, and quick games
- A profile menu for Product Memory, NETRA Coins, profile switching, personalization, settings, and theme color
- A creator-only six-tab workspace: Home, Create, Bites, CFund, Consumer, and Profile; Home and Bites share the consumer discovery experience
- Creator onboarding for identity, creative disciplines, aesthetic, experience, CFund goals, external presence, and availability
- Create Studio with a brief, creative concept, permitted formats, media staging, integration audit, escrow rights, and draft/submit actions
- Creator CFund Arena, consumer-journey analytics, and reputation / escrow earnings profile
- A full-screen, swipeable first-launch story using the supplied NETRA campaign artwork
- Original generated artwork for ad categories, creator stories, and the lead Bite

This is a local prototype. Profile details, preferences, follows, and demo activity are stored in the browser. The supplied Daily Mix background video plays locally; creator Bites, payments, accounts, and messaging services are not connected. Rewards are examples only.
